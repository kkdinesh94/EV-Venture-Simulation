Executive briefing

From your board

Three quarters in, the test market has given you a clear signal and an uncomfortable one. Commuter buyers rated your Surge model 78 out of 100 on brand judgment, the second-best score in the universe. That is the signal.

The uncomfortable part is that you served only 71% of the demand you created. Stock-outs cost you roughly 340 units of realised sales and, worse, they cost you the goodwill of buyers who walked into an experience centre and left empty-handed. Your market performance score carries that penalty directly.

Venture capital opens next quarter. What you show them is three quarters of evidence, not a forecast.

Two decisions dominate this quarter. First, whether to buy capacity now at Rs. 120 lakh per 500-unit block, ahead of the festive quarter multiplier. Second, whether to hold the Surge price at Rs. 92,000 or take the 4% cut that the price-elasticity estimate says would lift unit demand 11%.
