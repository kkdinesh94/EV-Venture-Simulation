How it works For faculty The platform
A competitive business simulation

Six firms enter the same market. Only the decisions are different.

Students form executive teams, design a product, price it, advertise it, staff it, manufacture it and finance it, quarter after quarter, against each other. The market settles the argument.

How a term runs

Four moves, repeated until the market stops rewarding them

01Read the market

Buy survey precision. Benefit ratings come back standardised to a mean of 100, with noise proportional to what the team was willing to spend. Interpretation is the work.

02Commit

Brand design, price, ad claims, sales force, production schedule, capital. Every member of a firm can edit; whoever locks last locks it for the team.

03Face the auditor

A negative pro forma cash flow, a claim the product cannot deliver, debt beyond the limit: the lock is blocked with a named reason before the quarter runs.

04Live with it

Results publish to every screen at once. Public market report, leaderboard, and a private financial pack that shows exactly which decision cost what.

For faculty

You can watch a team think, and step in before the quarter punishes them

Live trading floor

Every firm's state at a glance: locked, drafting live with a named editor, or not started. Force the run against a deadline when a team goes quiet.

Mentoring workspace

Open any firm's in-progress draft, read only. Coaching signals flag the mistake they have not noticed yet, so a two-minute conversation replaces a lost quarter.

Multiple sections at once

One deployment hosts unlimited universes side by side. Accounts carry across sections and terms. Co-teach by reassigning ownership, no shared logins.

Exports built for research

The complete decision log in long format, one row per firm per quarter, joined to results and the nine scorecard components. Loads straight into R.

The platform

Built so that a decision has a consequence you can trace

Demand responds to what each segment actually values, and segments do not value the same things. Production carries the real costs of capacity, variety and quality. Finance runs three statements with costing underneath, so a team can see which product and which location earned money and which only looked busy. Performance is a nine-measure balanced scorecard covering market, marketing, operations, people and financial risk.

Enrolled teams see exactly which measures they are judged on and how their own score moved from one quarter to the next. Instructors get the full breakdown for every firm, and an export of the complete decision history for grading and research.

Each cohort is pinned to a fixed version of the model when it is created, so a mid-term improvement can never rescore a league that is already running, and any quarter can be re-run exactly as it happened.
